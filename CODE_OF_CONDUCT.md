# Code of Conduct

Notch Player welcomes people of all backgrounds and experience levels. This applies to participation in project issues, pull requests, and other project spaces.

- Treat people with respect and keep feedback focused on the work.
- Make room for new contributors and explain unfamiliar project conventions.
- Respect privacy. Get consent before sharing someone else's personal information or private messages.
- Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, and personal attacks are unacceptable.

Maintainers may remove harmful content, lock discussions, or restrict participation. Use GitHub’s **Report content** menu for abuse that needs private handling. Please keep sensitive details out of public issues.
